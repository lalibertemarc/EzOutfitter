# EzOutfitter

## v1.0.1

- **Outfit switched back after a Look?** That's the outfit's situations (entering a city, combat and so on) changing it, which addons can't stop. Turn off that outfit's situations at a transmogrifier to keep it on. The Options page and README now say so.

## v1.0.0

- **Gear and outfit in one click.** A Look pairs one of your equipment sets with one of your transmog outfits. Click it and both change at once. Either half can be left alone, and "No outfit" shows your real gear.
- **Say it as you change.** Give a Look an optional /e line, such as *changes into travelling clothes.*, and it goes out with the swap. `%t` is your target's name.
- **Wherever you want it.** Use the small palette (key binding, launcher icon or `/eo`), the strip beside the character window, or a key binding per Look (Look 1 to 10, named after your Looks).
- **Know when you can change.** Changing outfit has a cooldown. Looks show it on their icon and tooltip, and clicking too early says how long to wait instead of changing only half of you.
- **Start from what you wear.** A new Look picks up your equipped set and shown outfit. The one you're wearing has a green border.
- First release for WoW Forever.
